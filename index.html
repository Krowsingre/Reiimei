          Security checklist

          • Turn on two-factor authentication for your GitHub account. Anyone who can change the app's code could read notes as you type them.
          • Turn on two-factor authentication for your Supabase account.
          • After creating your sync account, turn off new sign-ups in Supabase (Authentication, Sign In / Providers, Allow new users to sign up).
          • Use a different password for sync than for anything else.
          • Keep your iPhone and laptop locked with a passcode, and keep them updated.
