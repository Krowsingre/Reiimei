              Typing check
              
        
              For problems typing on a phone. Start it, open a practice note, then: dictate a sentence; hold Delete for a few seconds; tap in the middle of a word and type a word; press and hold a word and drag a handle. Come back here, tap Stop and send the file it gives. It records the keys, the letters next to the cursor and how the screen moves, so use a note with nothing private in it.
